🎙 Facilitator — During the Meeting

Live Notes

Copilot captures meeting notes in real time within the Teams pane. Every key point, decision, and question is logged automatically — no human scribe needed.

Intelligent Queue

Copilot tracks raised hands and speaking order, ensuring fair turn-taking. It surfaces who wants to speak next and keeps the conversation flowing.

Late-Join Recap

Participants who join late receive an instant AI-generated summary of what they missed, so they can contribute immediately without disrupting the flow.

Task Extraction

Action items are identified and assigned in real time as they are discussed. Tasks appear in the meeting pane with owners — no post-meeting guesswork.

How It Works

1
Join or start a Teams meeting with Copilot enabledCopilot activates automatically when the meeting begins. The Copilot icon appears in the meeting toolbar and side panel.
2
Open the Copilot pane during the meetingClick the Copilot icon to reveal the side panel. Notes, tasks, and summaries appear here in real time as the conversation unfolds.
3
Copilot captures notes & action items automaticallyAs participants speak, Copilot transcribes, summarizes, and extracts tasks. The Follow button keeps the panel synced to the live conversation.
4
Late joiners are caught up instantlyCopilot detects a late arrival and offers a Catch up summary of the meeting so far — key topics, decisions, and current discussion point.

🌐 Interpreter — Real-Time Cross-Language Communication

Speech-to-Speech Translation

Copilot translates spoken language in near real-time. A presenter speaking English is heard in Mandarin, German, or any supported language by every participant — each in their own tongue.

Voice Personalization

The interpreter uses a simulated voice that preserves the speaker's tone, pace, and emphasis — not a flat robotic translation, but a natural-sounding rendition.

Meeting Transcript

In addition to audio, Copilot provides a full multilingual transcript in the meeting pane. Every word is captured and searchable in the original and translated languages.

Global Team Enablement

Eliminates the need for human interpreters in routine meetings. Teams across China, Germany, and the US can collaborate seamlessly without language friction.

How It Works

1
Enable Interpreter in meeting optionsThe meeting organizer activates the Interpreter feature before or during the meeting. Language pairs are configured (e.g., English → Chinese, Chinese → English).
2
Each participant selects their preferred languageIn the meeting toolbar, each attendee chooses the language they want to hear. Copilot routes the translated audio stream individually.
3
Speaker talks; Copilot translates in real timeWhen a speaker begins, Copilot transcribes, translates, and synthesizes the audio in the listener's chosen language. Latency is kept to a minimum.
4
Transcript appears in the meeting paneThe full bilingual transcript is available alongside the audio. Participants can review what was said in either language after the meeting.
📸 Screenshot guide: Meeting options → Interpreter toggle → Language selector per participant → Bilingual transcript pane → Simulated voice indicator

⚡ Recap · Ask Copilot · Audio Recap — After the Meeting

Structured Recap

After the meeting ends, Copilot generates a comprehensive recap: meeting name, date/time, participants, AI-generated notes, follow-up tasks with assignees, and a chapter breakdown of topics discussed.

Ask Copilot

Query any past meeting with natural language. Ask “What did we decide about the Q3 budget?” or “Who was assigned the supplier audit?” — Copilot searches across all your meeting history.

Audio Recap

Generate a spoken summary of any meeting. Listen to key points, decisions, and action items as a podcast-style briefing — ideal for commute or multitasking.

Persistent Knowledge Base

All recaps, transcripts, and notes are stored in the meeting's chat thread and integrated with Microsoft Graph. Meeting intelligence becomes a searchable organizational asset, not a fleeting moment.

How It Works

1
Meeting ends → Recap auto-generatedCopilot processes the full transcript and produces a structured recap with chapters, key points, and action items. This appears in the meeting chat thread.
2
Review and refine the RecapParticipants can review, edit, and share the recap. Action items can be pushed to Planner, To Do, or any connected task system.
3
Ask Copilot follow-up questionsIn the meeting chat or Copilot chat, ask any question about the meeting — past or present. Copilot searches transcripts, recaps, and shared files to answer.
4
Generate an Audio RecapClick Audio Recap and Copilot produces a spoken summary. Share it with stakeholders who could not attend or listen on the go.

Enterprise Extensions

📋
License Model

Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on license (per user/month). Included with Microsoft 365 E3/E5 + Copilot, or standalone via CSP. Requires Teams Premium for select features.

🔒
Security & Compliance

All meeting data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Copilot respects existing Microsoft 365 compliance boundaries — retention policies, eDiscovery, and sensitivity labels apply to meeting intelligence.

🧠
Knowledge Management

Meeting recaps integrate with Microsoft Graph, SharePoint, and Viva Topics. Meeting intelligence is indexed and discoverable across the Microsoft 365 knowledge fabric — not siloed in Teams.
